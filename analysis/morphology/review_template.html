Draw independent phase annotations on real SEM crops. Algorithm overlays are available for comparison, but are not reference labels. Unknown or ambiguous regions can remain unlabelled.

Six development sites and five held-out sites, chosen from the known batches. This tests measurement methods after expert review; it does not establish unseen-batch accuracy. Crops and pixels do not add independent material samples.

Image context and method comparison

Definitions and evaluation

Current segmentation: site histogram thresholds, Gaussian σ=1 and one cross opening. Candidate hysteresis: seeds above the bright threshold +5, growth inside threshold −5 with four-neighbour connectivity, then the same opening. Cyan is void and gold is bright phase.

Width overlay: 2×distance to solid along a deterministic medial axis of ≥30 px² voids, excluding components touching the full image edge. Cyan/green corresponds to narrow widths; magenta corresponds to ≥100 px. The crop displays the full-site calculation. Expert ROI width comparisons instead exclude ROI-clipped components in both manual and predicted masks.

Partial manual masks: IoU, Dice and phase-fraction error only over labelled pixels. Fully labelled masks additionally support bright D50 and local-width error. Reviewed unmeasurable crops are abstentions. Empty classes are reported as unavailable rather than perfect scores. Lengths are pixels; scale calibration and chemistry are unconfirmed.
