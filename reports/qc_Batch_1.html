Limits of this dataset

Usable sample sizes per primary KPI (reference / batch): crack_frac 17/7; pore_max_d 17/7; pore_frac 17/7; bright_frac 17/5; bright_d50 17/5. Whether sites come from distinct specimens is unconfirmed — whether the sites of a batch come from distinct specimens has been asked of the organisers; n counts sites. The reference is heterogeneous: 4 grey-pore sites (71vgq3fw, kbdh4tri, tuy3zymq, x7u69zsw) rest on a fallback pore threshold and 3 cracked sites (0grcilhi, hzumfsms, ufdvpb81) carry the only clear material anomaly; 10 ordinary reference sites define the local-anomaly range. Minimum detectable change at 80 % power ranges from 1.75 to 3.00 reference MADs across the primary KPIs; smaller shifts cannot be ruled out. All measurements are 2-D sections, 25 nm/px nominal (TIFF export tag, unverified), chemistry of the bright phase unconfirmed. energy distance and drift score drop batch sites whose bright-phase KPIs are unusable (low-contrast). patch-level numbers localise evidence only and never enter n.
